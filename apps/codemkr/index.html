QR codes without the clutter

Create and download QR codes.

Make polished QR codes right in your browser. Everything is generated and exported on this device — nothing is uploaded.

Editable QR codes use an optional redirect service.

The QR stores a permanent redirect address. Updating its destination later does not change the printed QR image. This is off by default and requires an administrator to configure a redirect service.

Style
PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 2 MB. The image stays on this device. Test the finished QR before sharing.

Standard QR

The destination is stored directly in the QR. Generation and export stay entirely on this device.

Print safely

Keep a clear quiet zone, use strong contrast, and test the final printed code with more than one device before distributing it. Center images cover QR modules, so smaller is safer.

Center images

Adding a logo lowers how much of the code is readable, so high error correction is turned on automatically. Always scan-test before sharing.
