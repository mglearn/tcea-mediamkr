# Privacy

TCEA MediaMkr is designed to protect student and educator privacy.

## What we do

- **No accounts.** You do not create an account or sign in.
- **No tracking or advertising.** There are no analytics scripts or ad trackers.
- **No student data collection.** The tools do not ask for student names,
  emails, or other personal information.
- **Local processing.** Your files are opened, edited, and exported in your own
  browser. The tools are built to avoid uploading your media to a server.
- **Local downloads.** When you export, the finished file is saved to your
  device by your browser.

## Fonts

The pages load web fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display, DM Mono) from Google Fonts.
If your deployment requires zero third-party requests, the fonts can be
self-hosted; the layout falls back to system fonts automatically.

## Browser permissions

Some tools ask for a browser permission only when you choose a feature that
needs it — for example, AudioMkr requests microphone access when you start
recording, and screen capture is requested only when you use it. You can decline
these and still use the rest of the tool.

## Per-tool behavior

Each application documents its actual file, storage, and network behavior in its
own folder and in [`docs/privacy-inventory.md`](docs/privacy-inventory.md). We do
not claim that every operation is fully local unless it has been verified for
that specific tool.

## Contact
